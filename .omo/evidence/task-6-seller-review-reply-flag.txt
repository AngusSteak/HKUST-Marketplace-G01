  **Review**:
  a Buyer's rating from 1 to 5 with text on a completed Deal, one per Deal.

  **Seller Reply**:
  the Seller's single response to a Review, notified to the Buyer.

  **Flag**:
  the Seller's request for Admin attention on an inappropriate Review, distinct
  from Admin Hide (which removes a Review from public view) and Buyer Report
  Listing (which targets a Listing).
